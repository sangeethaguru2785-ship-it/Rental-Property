04 — Good to know

Rental questions,
answered.

Still deciding? A real advisor can walk you through availability, terms, and what living here actually feels like.

Ask an advisor
How do I book a tour?

Save the home you like, then choose Book a private tour or message an advisor. We arrange in-person and video tours seven days a week, usually within 48 hours.

What's included in the monthly rent?

Rent, building fees where they apply, and Stackly's advisory service. Furnishings, parking, and utilities are listed on each home, so there are no surprises at signing.

How long does an application take?

Most applications are reviewed within two business days. Once approved, leases are typically signed and keys handed over within a week.

Can I rent for less than a year?

Many homes offer three- to twelve-month terms. The available lease length is shown on each listing and confirmed during your tour.

Do you work with landlords?

Yes. We handle pricing, photography, vetting, and tenant matching. Start by choosing List your property on the contact page.
