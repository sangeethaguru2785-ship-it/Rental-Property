02 — Good to know

before you write.

The practical bits — response times, studio visits, and what to include in your note.

Call the studio
How soon will I hear back?

Within one business day, Monday to Friday. If your note arrives over the weekend, we'll reply first thing Monday morning.

Can I call instead?

Of course. Call +1 (123) 456-7890 during studio hours, Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm PT. If we miss you, we'll return your call the same day.

Do I need an appointment to visit?

No — drop in during studio hours for a coffee and a chat. If you'd like a dedicated advisor, mention it in your note and we'll set aside time.

What should I include in my message?

Your ideal move-in date, budget, preferred neighbourhoods, and whether you're renting or listing. A link to a property also helps.

Do you work with renters as well as landlords?

Yes. Renters pay no advisory fee; landlords pay an agreed letting fee. Both get the same named advisor from first conversation to keys.
