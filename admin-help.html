Good to know

Frequently asked

How do rent payouts work?

Collected rent is reconciled daily and paid to your nominated account on the next business day.

Can I add a teammate to this workspace?

Yes. Workspace owners can invite property managers and accountants with role-based access.

How are maintenance requests prioritised?

Requests are triaged by urgency and habitability impact. High-priority issues are escalated immediately.

Where can I export a rent roll?

Open Portfolio and use the rent-roll tools to export the current view for accounting.
